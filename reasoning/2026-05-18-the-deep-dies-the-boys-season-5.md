## Where I differ from base rate (and why)

I put The Deep at **88% YES**, above the 74/75 live book, for three linked reasons.

1. **Preseason leak track record.** MaxBlizz published a March 5 spoiler/leak article before the season aired that included The Deep among the reported deaths. That same broad leak set also included several deaths that are now reflected by aired-episode recaps and near-certain Polymarket marks, including A-Train, Black Noir, and Frenchie. It was not a clean single-source official leak, but it has enough partial validation to matter this close to the finale.
2. **Specific finale-leak claim.** A May 13 SoapCentral spoiler piece says leaks circulating online have The Deep dead very early in episode 8. The same piece is explicit rather than just assigning him a generic death flag. This may be derivative of the same leak ecology, so I do not treat it as independent proof, but it makes the `dies in finale` branch concrete.
3. **Episode 6-7 narrative setup corroborates the leak.** Episode 6/7 recaps have The Deep killing Black Noir II, causing an ocean disaster, being abandoned by Homelander / The Seven, and losing the sea creatures that were his remaining refuge. TheReviewGeek's episode 7 recap says the hammerhead shark Xander warns that Deep will be killed if he steps into water again. That is a very direct death mechanism for a finale that still needs fast payoffs.

The main counterargument is strong enough to keep this at 88%, not 95%: The Deep surviving as isolated, despised, and unable to enter water may be a better satirical punishment. ComicBook explicitly predicted he lives for that reason. Also, the leak sources are entertainment/blog/SEO sources, not screeners or official broadcast evidence. The market likely incorporates part of the same spoiler set already.

Still, the current price looks like it underweights the combination of partially validated preseason leaks plus the very specific episode-7 water-death setup. My fair failure probability is roughly 12%: leak wrong/changed, death left ambiguous, or show chooses humiliation over death.
