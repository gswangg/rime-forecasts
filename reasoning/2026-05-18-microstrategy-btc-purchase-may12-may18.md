## Where I differ from base rate (and why)

Two current signals make the >1,000 threshold look much too low:

1. **Saylor posted the weekly purchase hint.** On May 17, Michael Saylor posted the familiar Strategy purchase chart with the caption **"₿ig Dot Energy"**. Cointelegraph described this chart as one Saylor has consistently posted in the days ahead of a corporate purchase.
2. **The estimated size is far above the threshold.** BeInCrypto reported that STRC.live estimated roughly **15,466 BTC** accumulated across four active trading days last week, after STRC preferred share volume hit an all-time record of 15.1 million shares on Thursday. The same article says traders were waiting for Monday's 8-K to confirm one of Strategy's biggest weekly accumulation runs of 2026.

This market is not asking whether the STRC.live estimate is exact. It only needs an official/Saylor announcement above 1,000 BTC by the end of May 18 ET. If the estimate is even vaguely right, it clears by a very wide margin. A purchase-size estimate around 15k can be wrong by more than 90% and still clear the threshold.

My remaining failure cases:

- Strategy/Saylor does not make the official purchase announcement by 11:59 PM ET on May 18, even if a purchase happened.
- STRC.live's inference from preferred-share volume is structurally wrong this week.
- The Sunday Saylor chart is a generic treasury/proxy-vote promotion rather than a Monday purchase signal.
- An official announcement arrives but reports <=1,000 BTC, as happened in some smaller weeks.

I put those combined risks around 3%, so my forecast is **97% YES**.
